# Procedure de depot

Use this reference for Swiss filing procedure before the IPI.

## Filing Preparation

Collect the applicant identity, sign representation, sign type, goods/services list by Nice class, priority claim if any, and any color or figurative description needed for publication.

Use `filing_intake_check` for full filing preparation. The output should separate missing filing facts, warnings, and optional strategy questions.

## IPI Examination

The IPI checks formal requirements and absolute grounds. It does not examine relative grounds against earlier marks. Earlier-right conflicts can surface through opposition after publication or later civil proceedings.

## Costs and Timing

Always ask the user to verify current official fees and timelines on the IPI source at filing time. Treat any stored fee information as indicative only.

Use `filing_requirements_snapshot` for the stored fee/timing snapshot and official links. Include the snapshot date and do not present stored fees as a guarantee.

## Filing Output

Deliver:

- sign and filing type;
- recommended classes;
- final labels class by class;
- absolute-ground risk;
- anteriority-search plan;
- official-language status for the goods/services list;
- source/corpus date reminder;
- fee/timing snapshot and official links;
- post-filing watch items: publication, opposition window, and use requirement;
- next steps for e-trademark filing.

## Legal Disclaimer

Use a concise disclaimer in final answers: `Analyse preparatoire sur la base des corpus disponibles; a verifier au moment du depot et, en cas d'enjeu commercial significatif, avec un conseil specialise.`
