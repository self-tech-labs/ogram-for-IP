Privacy notice

Effective 24 September 2026

This notice describes the public Swiss Trademark Deposit MCP service at trademarks.ogram.swiss, operated by Parsing Sàrl (ogram), Switzerland. Contact: info@ogram.ch. The service uses Vercel for the public HTTPS domain and request forwarding, and Cloudflare for Worker processing and D1 reference-data storage.

Information processed

The service receives the arguments sent to its tools: proposed signs, business activities, goods and services wording, class numbers, search terms, and selected filing facts. Filing facts may include an applicant name, domicile country, and priority details. Tool results may repeat relevant supplied facts and include public trademark or examination records. Supply only information needed for the requested task. The service does not request payment data, identity-document numbers, passwords, account credentials, precise location, full conversation history, or unrelated files.

Purpose and recipients

Tool arguments are used to perform the requested lookup, classification, or preparatory check and return the result to your client. Vercel forwards requests from the branded domain to the Cloudflare service. Both hosting providers necessarily process connection metadata such as IP address and request timing; their infrastructure handles request traffic under their applicable policies and terms. See Vercel's privacy policy and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Your chosen client, including ChatGPT or Codex, processes conversation and returned results under its own policies and account settings. The MCP application does not call an inference API or send your query to IPI, Swissreg, or WIPO. Parsing Sàrl does not sell tool inputs or use them for advertising, profiling, or model training.

Storage and retention

The Cloudflare MCP application uses tool arguments and results in memory for the duration of a request. It does not save them to D1, R2, or an application request history. Application payload logging and Workers Observability logging are disabled. The D1 database contains the dated public reference corpus, not submitted filing facts.

Service rate limiting uses a shared 60-second counter at each Cloudflare location; its key contains no IP address, user identifier, or query text. Vercel and Cloudflare may retain infrastructure, security, and aggregate operational metadata under their own policies. The service does not claim that either provider retains no metadata. Support correspondence follows ogram's general privacy policy.

Location and protection

Requests use HTTPS. Vercel and Cloudflare may process them outside Switzerland; the reference database was placed using a Western Europe location hint. This is not a Swiss-only hosting or data-residency guarantee. Infrastructure administration is restricted to authorized operators.

Your controls

Omit names and confidential details when unnecessary, stop using the service, or disconnect the plugin in your client. The application keeps no request history to retrieve or delete after a request ends. Contact info@ogram.ch for access, correction, deletion, or objection requests concerning personal data otherwise held by us. Applicable rights, including a complaint to the Swiss FDPIC, remain available.

This website sets no application cookies and includes no analytics or advertising scripts. Material changes to this notice will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
